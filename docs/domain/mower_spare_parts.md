## Maintenance Intervals (General)
*   **Greasing:** Every 25–50 hours.
*   **Engine Oil:** Every 100–200 hours.
*   **Break-in Service:** First 50 hours (Change Hydraulic/Transmission filters).
*   **Hydraulic Oil:** Every 400–600 hours.
*   **Bedknife Grinding:** As needed (check for clean paper cut).
